## 2 · Duress · image: v4-2-duress.jpg

Someone forces you to open your wallet. What now?

Noxvault v4 lets you make a duress phrase.
Give it up and it pays out a small amount you chose, at once. Then the vault locks. Only your recovery key lifts it early.

Every vault stores a duress slot, so nobody can tell whether yours is real.

www.nox-vault.com/duress

---

## 3 · Sealed letters · image: v4-3-letters.jpg

Your testament moves the funds. But who tells your heirs where the other wallets are?

Sealed letters: written in your browser, encrypted to a date in the future (drand timelock), stored next to your vault.

While you're active, the date keeps moving. When you stop, they open.

Nobody can read them early. Not us. Not you.

www.nox-vault.com/letters

## 4 · Treasuries · image: v4-4-treasury.jpg

"Trust us, we won't dump the treasury."

Or: put it in a Noxvault and let holders check.

2-of-3 signers, a 72 h delay on every payout, vesting schedules. All in the vault address, so nobody can change them later.

Every treasury gets a public page and a badge for your site.

www.nox-vault.com/treasury

## 5 · Honest limits (reply under 1)

What v4 doesn't do:

• A duress phrase is no magic: anyone with a block explorer sees the real balance.
• A sealed letter opens at its date, also if you just forgot to refresh it.
• Everything is devnet and unaudited. Mainnet comes after an audit.
